# Folhagem do menu administrativo

Arquivo: `frontend/public/decor/sidebar-foliage.png`.

Gerado pela ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, com fundo transparente. PNG RGBA de 1024 × 1536 pixels, preservado sem recompressão. A aplicação usa a imagem inteira com `object-fit: contain`, opacidade de 46% e transição gradual no topo, somente na metade inferior do menu. Os textos permanecem em uma camada acima da imagem.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative botanical PNG for the bottom half of a narrow dark forest-green sidebar in the Ateliê Verde gardening app. Generate a new portrait composition, about 2:3 aspect ratio. Subject: lush realistic tropical green foliage with whole monstera deliciosa leaves, elegant philodendron leaves and a few slender palm fronds. Cluster the leaves near the bottom and the lower left edge, gradually sparse and airy toward the top. Use healthy vivid deep emerald and natural medium green tones, softly lit with subtle leaf texture, refined quiet botanical mood. Composition: every leaf must remain fully inside the image with a small clear margin, tall graceful arrangement, open negative space especially toward the upper right for menu labels to stay readable. Transparent background with genuine alpha, no rectangular background, no pots, no people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no interface. This is only a botanical asset, not a screenshot or a mockup. The app will apply mild opacity and a fade at the top; make the original leaves crisp and naturally saturated.
